# Update Log

## 2026-08-11
* **Creation**: Melee motion haptics now work. Physical swing and impact produce per-hand pulses on katana and machete, closing a gap the audio-derived layer cannot cover — the weapon whoosh falls outside its 28-320 Hz band and is stereo-derived, so it buzzes both grips. The CET weapon mod already detected swings; a `SetVRHapticPulse` native publishes hand, amplitude and duration into shared slots `[157..160]`, and a new bridge watcher consumes them through `HapticsEngine::Pulse` so the pulses mix with the audio and semantic layers instead of competing for the actuators. Requires a bridge build carrying the watcher. See [PSVR2 adaptive triggers and grip haptics](operations/psvr2-adaptive-triggers.md).
* **Fix**: Added the [overlay load-transition guard](fixes/overlay-load-transition-guard.md). Eight `DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_HUNG` faults, all stalling at the `FinalFlushBarriers` marker, were traced to the removal of the per-frame drain: a matched fresh-boot A/B ran clean for 6 minutes and 4 loads on the old drain and hung after 87 seconds and 1 load on middle-ground pacing. Restoring the drain only inside a bounded resource-churn window gives 70.3 fps median against 43.8 for the old drain, and a clean 8.8-minute run with 8 loads and zero fence timeouts.
* **Correction**: Marked [middle-ground frame pacing](fixes/overlay-middle-ground-pacing.md) unsafe on its own. Its validation section recorded no device fault, which was true of that single run and false of the configuration; one clean session did not establish safety.
* **Rejected hypotheses**: Concurrent Present workers corrupting backbuffer barriers, disproven by direct instrumentation — Cyberpunk serialises Present despite sixteen registered threads. A DLSS super-resolution/frame-generation version mismatch, disproven by reverting to the coherent stock 310.1 set and hanging anyway.
* **Fix**: Added the [crouch-preserving sprint gate](fixes/crouch-sprint-stance-gate.md). Sprint was derived from stick magnitude and asserted level-triggered, so ordinary forward movement on a Sense stick stood a crouched player up. Locomotion state now gates the assert; the crouch-sprint perk is declared through an F10 toggle rather than queried from TweakDB.
* **Correction**: Reversed the Sense weapon-category recommendation in [PSVR2 adaptive triggers and grip haptics](operations/psvr2-adaptive-triggers.md). The earlier preset profile was measured while Enhanced DualSense Support's UDP client was autostarting alongside the bridge; with only the bridge running the presets degrade haptics and the stock defaults are correct. UDP autostart is now locked off at the mod's default, its switch, and its config, because the default was `true` and any settings reset silently restored the conflict.
* **Finding**: Recorded that nothing besides the bridge may drive the Sense actuators. An OpenXR vibration action in the plugin cost most gun haptics even though every pulse was accepted by the runtime. Melee motion haptics remain unimplemented; the game-side detection and slots exist and the bridge already has a per-hand `Pulse` primitive to consume them.

Replaced the stale D-pad HUD-panning description with the current F10 layout and one-shot redscript design in [VR HUD layout](fixes/center-overlay-scaling.md), simplified the Sense adaptive-trigger explanation, added the [OKF maintenance and link policy](operations/okf-maintenance.md), and added `scripts/check_okf_links.py` to repair and validate document-relative links.

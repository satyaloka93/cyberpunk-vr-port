## 2026-08-25
* **Feature / `xr_fov_mode`, PSVR2 only**: Added a render-FOV sizing switch for the community's "very blurry picture" reports. Quantified on this PSVR2 first: eye frusta `L=-61.500 R=+43.446` (9.027 deg of cant), lens `HFov=104.946` — and cover sizing is `2*max(|L|,|R|)`, so the engine is asked for **123.000 deg**. At 3072 px that is 24.98 px/deg against 29.26 at the lens span, i.e. **a sixth of the linear resolution (37% of the solid angle) spent on frustum the wearer cannot see**. `xr_fov_mode=0` keeps cover sizing (default, unchanged); `1` asks for the de-canted span instead. Not free: the submit layer stays symmetric, so the wide side is short by the cant and an edge can appear — which is exactly the trade the black-border fix originally made. Implemented as a switch rather than a row in `kHeadsetFovDefaults` because that table's own rule is *measured, never guessed* and no single number serves both goals. **Gated on `IsRuntimePsvr2()`**: every other headset keeps shipped behaviour, since the Quest 3 already reports its true 94 deg span and a masked runtime (VDXR naming a Pico 4 "Oculus Quest2") must not be guessed at. `xr_force_fov` still overrides everything, and already offered this control undocumented — worth telling the thread asking for a slider that one exists.
